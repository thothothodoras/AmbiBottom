      LED strip & current

      Settings page (brightness, lamp positions) →
      The 45 was measured on the reference strip (BTF WS2812B ECO 30 LED/m, ~44 mA). Another strip: measure it (README: “Measuring mA per LED”).
      Hard limit, for the LEDs only. Power supply 900 mA → 800 leaves a margin.
      How many LEDs light, from the start of the strip. The rest stay off. At most what WLED’s “Length” says (the whole strip).
      The current each LED draws even when black.
      0 = old limit (everything dims together). Above 0: the LEDs between the lamps dim first and the peaks stay bright. 1 = the strongest.
      How fast an LED goes from 0 to 100 % while the picture plays. Dimming is immediate. 0 = no smoothing.
      When the TV image starts (or after Start / an interruption), the strip lights up gradually over this many seconds. 0 = as before (rise_ms).
      How much of each colour (0.2–1). The strip’s white is “cold” (bluish) next to the TV’s LEDs; for a warmer white lower B (e.g. 0.75) and G a little (e.g. 0.9). 1 / 1 / 1 = as before.
      “Keep”: the white balance corrects whites, greys and soft colours but does not dim a pure blue, red or yellow. “Everywhere” = as in v0.48.
      Small changes are made gradually (like the factory Ambilight) · the bigger the change the faster, and a scene change passes immediately. Bigger = more “relaxed”. 0 = as before.
      When the image goes completely dark (Ambilight off, end of a programme), the strip fades out over this many seconds. Normal changes stay immediate. 0 = abruptly.
      When the frame does not fit the limit, the peaks move back and forth ±that many LEDs around the lamps, so that the same LEDs are not always stressed. auto = the distance between two peaks (they pass over all LEDs). 0 = fixed peaks. Not yet tested by eye on the strip.
      Triangle: the same speed everywhere, equal time at each LED. Sine: as v0.23, it stays longer at the ends of the movement.
      How many seconds one full back-and-forth movement takes. Slow (e.g. 300) so it is not visible on the wall.
      0 = the peaks of the limit are at the lamps, as before. 2–8 = that many peaks at equal distances along the strip, not at the lamps. With the moving peaks they all move together. With a low “Peak emphasis” (0.2–0.3) they become slightly brighter points that move slowly. It acts only when the frame does not fit the limit. Not yet tested by eye on the strip.
      As the TV sees them: the bottom lamps (L2–L4 with 5 lamps, all with 3) spread from edge to edge of the strip, and the outermost LEDs get a bit of “edge colour” (the TV’s side). Through the edges: exactly v0.29 (outer-LED position, lamps at the edges, corners).
      Automatic: with 5 lamps L1 / L5 (the TV gives them to the sides), with 3 lamps JointSpace.
      Into this many LEDs from each end the edge colour enters, strongest at the first and less towards the middle. 0 = none.
      How much of the edge colour the first LED takes. 0.5 = half-and-half with the lamp. 1 = only the edge colour.
      How far in from each end of the strip the first and last lamp go. 0.25 = at the quarters, 0 = at the ends. Beyond these, the colour of the outer lamp is kept.
      Smooth: the colour changes gradually from lamp to lamp. Zones: each LED takes the nearest lamp. Zones + in-betweens: between two lamps a zone with their mix.
      For the zones: 0 = sharp boundaries, as before. E.g. 0.3 = each zone stays the same in its middle and at the boundaries slides to the next colour (30 % of the width). 1 = same as “Smooth blend”.
      With 5 lamps: L1 and L5 (the TV’s corners) go to the ends of the strip, L2–L4 to the positions of the 3 lamps. Then no corners from JointSpace enter and the limit peaks are only at L2–L4. With 3 lamps this does nothing.
      Mix: the edge takes the midpoint of the outer lamp and the bottom colour of the left/right side (JointSpace). Without JointSpace it keeps the lamp. Needs “Outer lamp position” above 0.
      When the TV stops sending, the strip holds the last frame for this long, then fades out.
      How long the fade to black takes. 0 = straight to black.
      That many seconds it sends black, then stops and WLED goes to its own preset (set WLED’s preset to off, see README).

      Checking settings…

      They apply immediately on the next frame and are saved in the ESP’s memory (they survive a restart and OTA). LED count = how many LEDs are used from the start of the strip · the rest stay off. In WLED (LED Preferences → Length) set all the LEDs the strip has. Values outside the limits are corrected by the bridge. The current is an estimate from these values, not a measurement.

      TV connection settings (JointSpace)

      JointSpace credentials for the three factory sides and the mix at the corners. The TV’s IP is found by itself from the Hue connection. The ESP asks the TV only while it is sending Hue.

      Pair with the TV: the ESP asks the TV for credentials; the TV shows a PIN on screen, you type it here, and they are saved by themselves. Or, if you already have credentials, enter them in the form below.

      Checking available settings…

      They are saved in the ESP’s memory (they survive a restart and OTA) and are never shown again. The password is not shown in the diagnostics.

Leave the page open while it records · it stops by itself after 10 minutes.

      Point the phone at the wall so that the glow at the side and bottom of the TV is visible, and drag the two boxes onto them (spots of the wall with the same colour). The page compares only the hue and corrects the “White balance” until they match. It measures only while the TV shows white / grey. The live camera needs https; otherwise “Photo”.
